💖 The 5 Hearts (Lives) Rule: You have exactly 5 Hearts. Normal minion attacks deplete your green HP bar. If HP drops to 0, you lose 1 Heart and respawn at the checkpoint with full HP.

💛 Restoring Lost Hearts: Seek rare Golden Hearts hidden in ruins or chests to restore lost heart containers up to the maximum of 5.
